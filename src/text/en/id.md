# usage

Provide an identity to sign your messages with.
Available commands:
  */id [identity]*
  */id auto* # Generate a new identity automatically

# created

Your identity is:
  *%identity%*
Your identifier is:
  *%identifier%*

# short

Your identity must be %min% characters or longer! To generate one automatically, use: /id auto

# accepted

Your identity was accepted.

# unsupported

This browser cannot sign messages (no Ed25519). Continuing unsigned.
